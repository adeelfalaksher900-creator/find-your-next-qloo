Sources & privacy

Your collection stays in this browser unless you enable cloud sync from Saved. Cloud sync encrypts interests and saved items in your browser and stores the encrypted copy in Supabase. The sync key stays on your device and is needed to connect another device; anyone with that key can access the collection. There is no email recovery. Game-night entries stay local. You can disconnect this device or delete the cloud copy from Saved. Manual downloads remain unencrypted and exclude game-night entries. Clear browser storage to remove local data.

When you request taste discovery, the interests you choose are sent to Qloo. Do not add personal information as an interest. Qloo results are associations, not guarantees.

To limit abuse, the server keeps a keyed hash of your IP address and a request count in private storage. The app does not store your raw address there. Old visitor counts are cleared on a later successful discovery request after the hourly window; the hosting provider may keep its own logs.

Catalog results are provided by TVmaze (CC BY-SA), Open Library, and CheapShark. PC prices are in USD and may change.

Qloo does not verify event schedules, tickets, product stock, streaming availability, or whether a creator is available to collaborate.
